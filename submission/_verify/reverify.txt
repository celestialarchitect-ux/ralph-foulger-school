=== GLOSSARY (corrected) ===
[Conversion] Using or misappropriating client trust funds for one’s own benefit. A license-law violation under HRS 467 that may also be criminal.
[Curtesy] At common law, a husband’s life interest in his deceased wife’s real property, historically conditioned on the birth of issue. Largely abolished today.
[Earnest money] Buyer's good-faith deposit, typically held by escrow.
[HARPTA] Hawai‘i Real Property Tax Act (HRS §235-68): a 7.25% withholding on the amount realized when a non-resident sells Hawai‘i real property — a prepayment of state income tax on the gain, distinct from the county real-property (ad valorem) tax.
[Eviction (Hawaii)] The legal process to remove a tenant: proper written notice, then court summary possession (HRS 521/666). | HI: As of Feb 5, 2026 (Act 278), residential nonpayment requires 10 days’ written notice (with mediation if the tenant requests); other curable breaches require 10 days under HRS §521-72.
[Constructive eviction] Landlord conduct so severe tenant must leave; treated legally as eviction.
[Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) handling] A buyer’s good-faith deposit submitted with an offer and held in trust. In Hawai‘i a broker must deposit trust funds (including earnest money) into a federally insured trust account or neutral escrow by the next business day after receipt (HAR §16-99-4). | HI: Late deposit of trust funds is grounds for license discipline.
