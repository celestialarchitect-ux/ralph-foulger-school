Hawaiʻi Real Estate Salesperson License
One book. The entire path to your license.

This is the complete textbook for the Hawaiʻi Real Estate Salesperson License — the only volume a candidate needs. The full course of study, the entire question bank with every answer explained, the real-estate mathematics, and a complete glossary are gathered here in a single book, written and taught by an instructor with more than fifty years in Hawaiʻi real estate.

Inside this volume
  • Twenty complete chapters — eleven national, nine Hawaiʻi — every lesson and key concept in full
  • 2,290 practice questions, each with all options, the correct answer, and a full explanation
  • Every exam math type, worked step by step
  • A complete real-estate glossary, with the Hawaiʻi terms set apart
  • Aligned to the State of Hawaiʻi Real Estate Commission exam blueprint

Ralph S. Foulger has been licensed in Hawaiʻi as a salesperson since 1972 and as a broker since 1987, holds the CPM designation, and is a state-certified instructor across all three license tracks. He founded the Academy in 1996 and has taught Hawaiʻi real estate for three decades.
